# Spec Kit Extensions Roadmap

This roadmap outlines the strategic development of high-performance extensions for Spec Kit, focusing on engineering discipline, governance, and verification for AI agents.

## Strategic Alignment
This roadmap is built on the **"Governance > Magic"** core assumption. It directly addresses the fatal flaws of AI engineering:
1. **Friction vs. Speed**: Solved via Phase 1 (Insta-Set).
2. **Memory Drift/Vibe Coding**: Solved via Phase 2 (MemoryLint 2.0).
3. **Lack of Rigor**: Solved via Phase 3 (Semantic Critique).
4. **Framework Lock-in**: Solved via Phase 4 (Universal Bridge).

## Scenario Evolution (Goal Direction)
We are moving the "Scenario" from simple code assistance to **Managed AI Engineering**:
- **Current**: Individual dev experimenting with SDD.
- **Phase 1-2**: Professional dev managing complex architecture projects with high predictability.
- **Phase 3**: Teams enforcing engineering standards through automated gates (CI/CD).
- **Phase 4**: Enterprise-grade, framework-agnostic AI governance.

## Phase 1: Stability & Trust (P0)
**Focus**: Lowering entry barriers and ensuring the reliability of discipline enforcement.

- **Insta-Set Installer**: One-line installation and setup.
- **Hook Robustness**: Diagnostic tools and dry-run modes.
- **Evidence-Based Archiving**: Automatic storage of verification artifacts.

## Phase 2: Deep Governance & Memory Defense (P1)
**Focus**: Solving AI "hallucination" and "memory drift" at the source.

- **MemoryLint 2.0**: Semantic auditing of instructions.
- **Live-Guard Constitution**: Real-time enforcement during planning and implementation.
- **Context Heatmaps**: Token optimization and rule pruning.

## Phase 3: Audit & Automated Closure (P2)
**Focus**: Moving from assistance to automated Quality Assurance.

- **Semantic Critique**: Deep requirement-to-code alignment checks.
- **Auto-Respond**: Generating fix plans from critique output.
- **CI/CD Gates**: Mandatory verification signatures for PRs.

## Phase 4: Multi-Agent Synergy & Ecosystem (P3)
**Focus**: Building the long-term moat for SDD.

- **Agent Collaboration Protocol (SAP)**: Synchronized rules for multi-agent projects.
- **Universal Bridge**: Adapting SDD discipline to other platforms (Cursor, Windsurf, LangGraph).
- **Extension SDK**: Allowing the community to build their own SDD-compliant gates.
